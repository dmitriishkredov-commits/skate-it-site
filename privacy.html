Privacy Policy

Skate-It! · Last updated November 16, 2026

Summary

Skate-It! does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

What the app uses

The game reads your iPhone's motion sensors (gyroscope and accelerometer) to recognize tricks. This data is processed on your device in real time and is never sent anywhere.

Your settings, score and stats are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No internet connection is used

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
